Temperatures and dew points are in \degF; the pressure is the three-digit code and the decoded value in hPa; cloud cover is in percent (the filled fraction of the circle); the wind is the direction it comes \textbf{from} and its speed in knots (counted from the barbs, so approximate). Positions are good to about 10\,km. A dash means that no value is available (station 9 has no dew point).
